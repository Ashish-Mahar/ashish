About Me

I'm Ashish Mahar, a BCA 3rd-year student at LSM Campus, Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand. My journey started in Class 10 — not on a laptop, but on a mobile phone. What began as curiosity quickly became a passion for building things.

Today, I build AI-powered websites, mentor students in robotics, and explore cyber security & IoT. I started teaching robotics at just 17.5 years old — and that decision changed how I see technology: not just as code, but as a way to inspire others.

When I'm not building websites or teaching, you'll find me tinkering with Arduino, experimenting in Kali Linux, or designing my next big idea — like a self-driving car.
